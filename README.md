## Why this line should win (judged against the comp's own brief, in order)

1. **About office life, lands as a joke** — a burnout email to HR, answered by HR itself.
2. **True to the office** — burnout emails to HR and 30-minute calendar invites are universal office furniture; the listener nods before laughing.
3. **Surprising turn** — the last three words are the punchline: HR answers a burnout plea by scheduling burnout itself. A fresh angle, not a pun or a stock line.
4. **Tight** — the "I emailed HR / HR replied" parallel carries the whole joke; no word wasted, reads in one breath.
5. **Original** — HR answering burnout with a calendar slot is not a familiar line with the nouns swapped.
